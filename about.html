About Ara Tax Services LLC

We are a private tax preparation firm in San Ramon, California. We prepare and file United States federal tax reporting for people who have cryptocurrency activity.

What we do

You send us the records of what you did with digital assets during the year. We reconcile them, work out the gain, loss and income they produce, and report the result on the federal forms it belongs on — usually Form 8949 and Schedule D for disposals, Schedule 1 line 8v for ordinary income, Schedule C where the activity is a business, and Form 1040-X where a past year has to be amended.

The IRS treats digital assets as property, not currency. That single rule is what turns a swap, a purchase paid for in crypto and a gas fee into events that have to be worked out and reported. This site is about federal reporting.

How we work

  1. You tell us the scope

    Six short questions. No Social Security number, no date of birth, no bank or card details, no wallet balances, no uploads.

  2. We read your records before we quote

    If something needed is missing, we tell you before any work starts — not halfway through.

  3. You get a written quote first

    Priced on the work involved. Nothing is charged until you accept it.

  4. A person prepares the return

    The team includes experienced tax preparers and specialists who work with the IRS filing system. Software imports and matches transactions; it is not left to decide how an item is treated.

  5. You review a draft before filing

    We show you which transactions produced which numbers. Nothing is filed until you say go ahead.

How your records reach us

Read-only exports and public wallet addresses only. We do not accept records by email — when work starts we set up a channel for the transfer and tell you how to use it.

We are not the IRS

Ara Tax Services LLC is a private tax preparation firm. We are not the IRS. We are not affiliated with, endorsed by, or acting on behalf of the Internal Revenue Service, the U.S. Department of the Treasury, or any government agency.

You can file your own federal tax return directly with the IRS at no cost to you, including through IRS Free File if you are eligible. Ours is a paid, optional alternative in which we do the work for you. If your year was straightforward, doing it yourself is a reasonable thing to do.

What we do not do

  • No custody. We never hold, move or have any authority over your assets, and there is nowhere on this site to enter a key.
  • No investment or trading advice. No coin views, no rankings of exchanges or wallets.
  • No collection work. Catch-up filings and amendments are ordinary preparation and we do them. Negotiating a balance owed is a different business.
  • No promised results, and no published prices. You see a quote after we have seen the scope.

You will not find a client count, a founding year, an award badge or a client quote anywhere on this site. Nothing about a history has been invented to fill that space.
