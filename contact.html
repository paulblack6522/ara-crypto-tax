Do not send tax documents or account credentials by email

Email is not a safe place for tax records. Do not attach a return, a statement, an exchange export or an identity document to an email to us, and do not type an account number, a password, an API key or a recovery phrase into one. When work starts we set up a channel for the file transfer and tell you how to use it before you send anything.

Before you write to us

We will never ask for your seed phrase, private keys, exchange passwords, or API keys. No one legitimate ever will.

If you want a quote

Start with the intake form

The intake form asks about the tax years involved, the venues and chains you used, the kinds of activity in the year, the state of your records and your prior filings. It uses bands rather than exact figures, and it asks for no Social Security number, no date of birth, no bank or card details and no uploaded documents. Once we have read it we tell you what is missing and send a written quote. Nothing is charged until you accept it.
