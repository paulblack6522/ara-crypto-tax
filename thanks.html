We have your details

Thank you. One of our tax preparers will contact you to confirm what we need and to give you a written quote before any work begins.

What happens next

  1. A preparer reads your answers and replies by the channel you asked for, email or phone.
  2. If anything in your answers changes the scope of the work, we ask about it then, not later.
  3. You get a written quote. Nothing is charged, and no work starts, until you have read that quote and told us to go ahead.

There is no reference number to keep and nothing further for you to do right now.

While you wait

If you want to get your records together in the meantime, what we need from you lists the exports and details the work usually depends on. Please do not email tax documents as attachments. If we take the work on, we will set up a channel for that with you.

If you reached this page by accident

This page is the confirmation we show after the request form is sent. If you arrived here from a link or a bookmark, nothing has been submitted and we have not received anything from you.

  • To send us your details, go to start your filing.
