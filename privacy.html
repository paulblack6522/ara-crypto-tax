Privacy policy

This page describes what Ara Tax Services LLC does with information, on this website and in the work we do for clients. It is written to match what the site actually does, not what a template says.

Last updated August 1, 2026.

The short version

This website sets no cookies, runs no analytics, and loads nothing from any other company. Nothing you type into a form on this site is transmitted anywhere in this build. We never ask for your seed phrase, private keys, exchange passwords or API keys. We do not sell or share personal information.

1. Who this policy is from

Ara Tax Services LLC is a private tax preparation firm in San Ramon, California. We prepare and file United States federal tax reporting for people who have cryptocurrency activity. This policy covers this website and the information we handle when we work with a client.

Questions about this policy go to hello@aratax-example.com or (925) 555-0148.

2. What this website does today

Written plainly, so you can check it yourself with your browser's developer tools:

  • No cookies. The site sets none. There is no consent banner because there is nothing to consent to.
  • No analytics and no trackers. There is no Google Analytics, no tag manager, no advertising pixel, no heatmap tool and no session recorder anywhere on this site.
  • No third-party requests. Every stylesheet, script, font and image comes from this site itself. There are no web fonts loaded from another company, no maps, no video embeds, no chat widget and no captcha. The site works with no network connection after the page has loaded.
  • The forms send nothing. In this build, the request form and the contact form are handled entirely inside your own browser. Their contents are not transmitted to us or to anyone else, and they are not written to a database.
  • No browser storage. The site writes nothing to sessionStorage or localStorage. What you type into a form stays in the page you are looking at, and is gone when you close or reload it.
  • No fingerprinting. We do not build a device or browser profile, and we do not attempt to identify you across sites.

The site is served as static files by a third-party hosting provider. Like any web host, it may keep ordinary server request logs, which can include an IP address, the time of the request, the page requested and the browser user agent. Those logs exist for delivering and protecting the service. We do not receive them, we do not read them, and we do not use them for marketing or measurement.

3. What we never ask for

We will never ask for your seed phrase, recovery phrase, private keys, exchange passwords, or exchange API keys or secrets. No one legitimate ever will. If anyone contacts you claiming to be from this firm and asks for any of those, it is not us.

The forms on this website also contain no field for a Social Security number, an ITIN, an EIN, a date of birth, a bank account or routing number, a card number, a driver's licence, a passport, or a document upload. Not as an optional field. If you are looking at a page that asks you for one of those under our name, close it and call us.

To prepare a return we do eventually need identifying information that the IRS requires on the forms, such as taxpayer identification numbers. We collect that only after you have engaged us, through a channel we set up with you directly. Never through this website. Never by ordinary email.

4. Information you choose to give us

If you use the request form or the contact form, the fields ask for:

  • Your name, your email address, and a phone number if you choose to give one.
  • Which tax years you are asking about and what you want done, such as a full return, a calculation, an amendment or a review.
  • Scope information about your cryptocurrency activity: how many exchanges and wallets are involved and which ones by name, which kinds of activity happened during the year, and a broad band for the number of transactions.
  • What you filed in prior years, how you would like us to reply, and a time that suits you for a preparer to call.

We ask for bands rather than exact figures wherever a band is enough to scope the work. The exchange and wallet question asks for names only. It does not ask what you hold on them, what any of it is worth, or for a login, an API key or a recovery phrase — and it never will. We ask which platforms you used because that is what tells a preparer which records exist and in what format.

We use what you send us to answer your question, to work out what the job involves, and to send you a written quote. We do not use it for advertising, we do not build a profile from it, and we do not add you to a marketing list you did not ask for.

5. Once you become a client

When an engagement begins, the information involved changes character. It becomes tax return information, and we treat it as confidential from the first document.

  • How records reach us. We set up a channel for documents with you at the start of the engagement. We do not ask clients to send tax documents as ordinary email attachments, and we ask you not to.
  • What we use it for. Preparing and filing the return you engaged us for, and nothing else. We do not disclose or use client tax return information for any other purpose unless you direct us to in writing, or unless the law requires it.
  • Who sees it. Only the people at this firm who are working on your return. Where we use a service provider to do the work, such as professional tax preparation software or a document exchange service, that provider is bound to handle the information only for us and only for that purpose.
  • What we do not do. We do not sell client information. We do not share it with advertisers, data brokers, lenders, exchanges, or anyone offering you another product.
  • Retention. We keep client records for seven years from the due date of the return they relate to, and then destroy them. Paper is shredded and digital copies are deleted from live systems and from backups on their normal cycle. If you ask us to delete something sooner, we will do it wherever we are not required to keep it.
  • Legal process. If we ever received a valid legal demand for client records, we would comply with it, and we would tell you unless we were prohibited from doing so.

6. Security

Records are kept on access-controlled systems, encrypted in transit and at rest, with access limited to the people working on the file. No system is perfect, and we do not claim otherwise. What we can control is how little we ask for in the first place, and this site asks for as little as the work allows.

If you believe information you gave us has been exposed, contact us at hello@aratax-example.com and we will investigate.

7. Children

This website is meant for adults dealing with their own tax reporting. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has sent us information, write to hello@aratax-example.com and we will delete it.

8. Your California privacy rights

This section applies to California residents under the California Consumer Privacy Act as amended by the California Privacy Rights Act. It describes our practices over the 12 months before the date at the top of this page.

These are the statutory categories. Because this build transmits nothing from its forms, most of the collection described here happens only if you contact us directly or become a client.

8.2 Sources, purposes and disclosures

We collect personal information from one source: you. We do not buy lists, we do not append data from brokers, and we do not collect information about you from other companies.

We use it to respond to your enquiry, to scope and quote the work, to prepare and file the return you engaged us for, to keep the records a tax preparer is expected to keep, and to meet our own legal obligations. We disclose it only to service providers who work on our behalf under contract, and only for those purposes.

We do not sell personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioural advertising. We have not done so in the preceding 12 months. That includes information about anyone we know to be under 16.

We do not use sensitive personal information for any purpose other than preparing the return you asked us to prepare, so the right to limit its use does not restrict anything we do with it.

8.3 Your rights

  • Right to know. You may ask what categories and specific pieces of personal information we have collected about you, where it came from, why we collected it, and who we disclosed it to.
  • Right to delete. You may ask us to delete personal information we collected from you. We will do it, except where we are required to keep the record, for example where a filed return and its supporting records must be retained.
  • Right to correct. You may ask us to correct inaccurate personal information about you.
  • Right to opt out of sale or sharing. There is nothing to opt out of. We do not sell or share personal information, so this site carries no "Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information" link.
  • Right to limit use of sensitive personal information. Available on request, although as described above we already use it for one purpose only.
  • Right to non-discrimination. We will not deny you service, charge you a different amount, give you a lower quality of service, or treat you differently in any way because you exercised a privacy right.

8.4 How to exercise a right

Email hello@aratax-example.com with the words "California privacy request" in the subject line, or call (925) 555-0148. Tell us which right you are exercising.

We will confirm we received the request within 10 business days and respond within 45 days. If we need longer we will tell you why before the 45 days are up, and we will take no more than an additional 45 days.

Before we act on a request to know, delete or correct, we have to be reasonably sure the request is really from you. We will ask you to confirm details we already hold. We will not ask you to create an account to make a request, and we will not use the information you give us for verification for anything else.

An authorised agent may make a request for you. We will ask for written permission signed by you, and we may still ask you to verify your own identity directly with us.

9. Other states

If you live in a state with its own consumer privacy statute, you may have comparable rights to access, correct or delete your personal information. Use the same contact route in section 8.4 and we will handle your request the same way.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change how this site or this firm handles information, we change this page at the same time, not afterwards, and we update the date at the top. If the change is significant and we hold your email address as a client, we will tell you directly.
