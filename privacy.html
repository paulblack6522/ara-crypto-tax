Privacy policy

This page describes what Ara Tax Services LLC does with information, on this website and in the work we do for clients. It is written to match what the site actually does, not what a template says.

Last updated August 1, 2026.

The short version

This website sets no cookies, runs no analytics, and loads nothing from any other company. Nothing you type into a form on this site is transmitted anywhere in this build. We never ask for your seed phrase, private keys, exchange passwords or API keys. We do not sell or share personal information.

1. Who this policy is from

Ara Tax Services LLC is a private tax preparation firm in San Ramon, California. This policy covers this website and the information we handle when we work with a client. Questions go to hello@aratax-example.com or (925) 555-0148.

2. What this website does

You can check all of this yourself with your browser's developer tools:

  • No cookies, so there is no consent banner.
  • No analytics and no trackers — no tag manager, advertising pixel, heatmap or session recorder.
  • No third-party requests. Every stylesheet, script and image comes from this site. The site works with no network connection once the page has loaded.
  • The forms transmit nothing. They are handled entirely inside your own browser and are not written to any database.
  • Browser storage. The step-by-step filing form on Start your filing keeps your progress in sessionStorage, which lives on your own device only. It is never sent anywhere, and your browser clears it when you close the tab. Nothing else on the site writes to storage, and no localStorage is used.
  • No fingerprinting. We do not build a device profile or track you across sites.

The site is served as static files by a third-party host, which like any host may keep ordinary server request logs. We do not receive, read or use them.

3. What we never ask for

We will never ask for your seed phrase, recovery phrase, private keys, exchange passwords, or exchange API keys or secrets. No one legitimate ever will. If anyone contacts you claiming to be from this firm and asks for any of those, it is not us.

The forms here also contain no field for a Social Security number, ITIN or EIN, a date of birth, a bank or card number, a driver's licence, a passport, or a document upload — not even as an optional field. To prepare a return we do eventually need the identifying information the IRS requires; we collect that only after you have engaged us, through a channel we set up with you. Never through this website, and never by ordinary email.

4. Information you choose to give us

The forms ask for your name, email and optionally a phone number; which tax years and what you want done; scope information about your activity — which exchanges and wallets by name, the kinds of activity, and a band for the number of transactions; what you filed in prior years; and a time for a call.

The exchange and wallet question asks for names only. It does not ask what you hold on them, what it is worth, or for a login, an API key or a recovery phrase — and it never will. We ask which platforms you used because that tells a preparer which records exist and in what format.

We use what you send to answer your question, scope the work and send a written quote. We do not use it for advertising, build a profile from it, or add you to any marketing list.

5. Once you become a client

The information becomes tax return information and we treat it as confidential from the first document.

  • How records reach us. Through a channel we set up at the start of the engagement, never as ordinary email attachments.
  • What we use it for. Preparing and filing the return you engaged us for, and nothing else, unless you direct otherwise in writing or the law requires it.
  • Who sees it. Only the people here working on your return, plus any service provider bound to handle it only for us and only for that purpose.
  • What we do not do. We do not sell client information or share it with advertisers, data brokers, lenders or exchanges.
  • Retention. Seven years from the due date of the return, then destroyed. If you ask us to delete something sooner we will, wherever we are not required to keep it.
  • Legal process. We would comply with a valid legal demand, and would tell you unless prohibited.

6. Security

Records are kept on access-controlled systems, encrypted in transit and at rest, with access limited to the people working on the file. No system is perfect and we do not claim otherwise — what we control is how little we ask for in the first place. If you believe information you gave us has been exposed, contact us and we will investigate.

7. Children

This site is meant for adults dealing with their own tax reporting. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has sent us information, write to hello@aratax-example.com and we will delete it.

8. Your California privacy rights

This section applies to California residents under the California Consumer Privacy Act as amended by the California Privacy Rights Act (CCPA/CPRA), and describes our practices over the 12 months before the date at the top of this page.

8.2 Sources, purposes and disclosures

We collect from one source: you. We do not buy lists or append data from brokers. We use it to answer your enquiry, scope and quote, prepare and file your return, keep the records a preparer is expected to keep, and meet our legal obligations. We disclose it only to service providers working on our behalf under contract, for those purposes.

We do not sell personal information and we do not share it for cross-context behavioural advertising, and have not in the preceding 12 months — including for anyone we know to be under 16. We use sensitive personal information for one purpose only: preparing the return you asked for.

8.3 Your rights

  • Know what we collected about you, where it came from, why, and who we disclosed it to.
  • Delete what we collected from you — except where we are required to keep it, such as a filed return and its supporting records.
  • Correct inaccurate information.
  • Opt out of sale or sharing. There is nothing to opt out of, which is why this site carries no “Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information” link.
  • Limit use of sensitive personal information. Available on request, though we already use it for one purpose only.
  • Non-discrimination. We will not deny you service, charge you differently, or treat you differently for exercising a privacy right.

8.4 How to exercise a right

Email hello@aratax-example.com with “California privacy request” in the subject, or call (925) 555-0148, and tell us which right you are exercising.

We confirm receipt within 10 business days and respond within 45 days; if we need longer we will tell you why before the 45 days are up and take no more than another 45. We will ask you to confirm details we already hold so we can be reasonably sure the request is from you — we will not make you create an account, and we will not use verification information for anything else. An authorised agent may make a request with written permission signed by you.

9. Other states

If your state has its own consumer privacy statute you may have comparable rights. Use the same route in section 8.4 and we will handle it the same way.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change how this site or this firm handles information, we change this page at the same time, not afterwards, and update the date at the top. If the change is significant and we hold your email address as a client, we will tell you directly.
