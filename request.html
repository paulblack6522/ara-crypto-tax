Crypto tax filing, prepared and filed for you

Answer six short questions about your cryptocurrency activity and pick a time to talk. A preparer calls you at the time you choose, walks through what your return needs, and sends a written quote. No documents needed yet.

  • A preparer reviews your answers — every return is prepared by a person.
  • You get a written quote before any work begins.
  • Nothing is charged until you read the quote and tell us to go ahead.

This takes about a minute. It asks about the shape of your activity, not exact figures — a band or a “not sure” tells us more than a blank field. It does not ask for a Social Security number, a date of birth, a bank account, a card number, wallet balances, or a document upload.

About your activity
1. Which tax year or years do you need help with? * Tick every year that needs work.
2. What kind of crypto activity did you have? * Tick all that apply. This tells the preparer which forms your return will need.
3. Roughly how many transactions in total? * A rough band across all years and platforms is fine.
4. How many exchanges and wallets did you use? * A rough count across all years is enough.
5. Which exchanges or wallets did you use? * Tick all that apply, across every year. This tells a preparer which records exist and in what format. We do not ask what you hold on them, and we never ask for a login, an API key or a recovery phrase.

Names only. If one has closed down or you cannot get into it, say so here — that is a normal situation and a preparer will tell you what can be reconstructed.

6. Have you reported crypto on a past return before? * If some years were missed, a preparer can look at amended returns. This is a normal question, not a judgement.

What happens after you send this

  1. A preparer calls you

    At the time you chose, a preparer goes through your answers and works out exactly what your return needs. Our team includes experienced specialists who work with the IRS filing system, so complex returns are handled by someone who does this work. No documents needed for this call.

  2. You get a written quote

    Priced on the work involved. Nothing is charged until you accept it.

  3. We prepare and file

    You send your records through a secure channel we set up — never by plain email. You review a draft before anything is filed.

You are in control

We are not the IRS, and we do not take custody of your assets or your keys. You can always file your own federal return directly with the IRS for free. This is a paid, optional alternative in which we do the work for you.
