How it works

This page sets out the whole engagement in order, from the first form you fill in to the copies you keep. Read it before you start so you know what we ask for and when.

The seven steps

Every engagement runs in the same order. You can stop at any step before you accept the quote, and nothing is charged before then.

  1. You tell us what you did

    Five short steps: the tax years, where the activity happened, what kind it was, your records and prior filings, and how to reach you. If you would rather talk first, the callback form asks six questions instead and books a time. No documents needed yet, and neither one ever asks for a Social Security number, a date of birth, a seed phrase, a key, a password or an API key.

  2. A preparer reviews it and contacts you

    By email or phone, whichever you asked for. If anything changes the scope, we ask then rather than later.

  3. You get a written quote before any work starts

    It states the years covered, the forms we will prepare, and what is outside the scope. Nothing is charged until you accept it.

  4. You send your transaction records

    Exports, public addresses and statements go through a secure channel we set up — never plain email. Read-only records only; we never take custody of your assets.

  5. We reconstruct basis and prepare the forms

    We rebuild cost basis, reconcile transfers between wallets you own, and prepare Form 8949 and Schedule D with the rest of your return. See the guide to the forms.

  6. You review a draft

    The full draft with the digital asset schedules, plus a plain summary of how each figure was arrived at. Nothing is filed until you have read it and signed the authorization.

  7. We file, and you keep everything

    Copies of every form filed plus the working papers behind them — the ledger, the basis calculations and the source records. Yours to keep.

If your records are incomplete

That is ordinary. Exchanges close, wallets get abandoned, and the statements that do arrive are thinner than people expect. A 2025 Form 1099-DA generally shows proceeds but not basis — the IRS states basis must be calculated by taxpayers before the return is filed — and for DeFi, self-custody, non-US exchanges, staking and mining, no statement arrives at all. The duty to report does not depend on receiving one.

So we work from what can be evidenced: on-chain history from your public addresses, exchange exports, and matching each withdrawal to its deposit so a transfer between your own wallets is not read as a sale. Where a gap remains we tell you what is missing and what it affects before work starts.

Some gaps have a fixed consequence rather than a judgment call: basis is tracked per wallet and per account from January 1, 2025; without adequate identification the default is first in, first out within that wallet; and for a gift with no documentation of the donor's basis, basis is zero.

What we do not do

  • No investment advice. We do not rate coins, exchanges or wallets, or comment on prices.
  • No custody of assets or keys. Never a seed phrase, private key, password or API key.
  • No bookkeeping unless you engage us for it separately.
  • No representation before the IRS. That is a separate engagement requiring an authorized practitioner.

If what you need falls outside that list, say so on the request form and we will tell you before you accept a quote.
