What we need from you

The records this engagement runs on, item by item, with the reason each one is asked for. You do not need any of it to fill in the request form. Gather it after we have scoped the work.

We never ask for keys or passwords

We will never ask for your seed phrase, private keys, exchange passwords, or API keys. No one legitimate ever will. Every item on this checklist is a read-only record: a file you export, a public address, a statement you were sent, or a copy of a return you already filed. If anyone contacting you about your return asks for a recovery phrase, a private key, a login or API access, stop and call us on (925) 555-0148.

Tick each item as you gather it. The ticks are for your own use — nothing here is saved or sent. Not every line applies to every filer; send what you have.

Exporting your history

Every venue lays this out differently, so this stays general. Look for the account area named for reports, statements or taxes. Ask for the full transaction history for the whole calendar year — a summary or a year-end balance is not enough to rebuild basis from. Choose CSV where it exists, export each account and year separately, and include trades, conversions, deposits, withdrawals, fees and rewards.

Do not create an API key for us, and do not send screenshots in place of an export. If the only way to produce a record is to give someone access to your account, tell us instead.

You do not need any of this yet

The request form comes first and asks no document of you. Records are collected after the scope is agreed and a written quote is accepted.
