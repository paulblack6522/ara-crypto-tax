Before you start

We will never ask for your seed phrase, private keys, exchange passwords, or API keys. No one legitimate ever will.

This form does not ask for a Social Security number, an ITIN, a date of birth, a bank account, a card number, or a document upload. If identifying details are needed later, a preparer collects them from you over a secure channel.

Step 4 of 5 — Records and prior filings

What you already have decides how much reconstruction the return needs.

What accounting method have you used in prior years?
Did you make a Rev. Proc. 2024-28 basis allocation (a wallet-by-wallet snapshot as of January 1, 2025)? Since January 1, 2025 basis is tracked per wallet and per account rather than in one pool. That safe harbor allocation is irrevocable, so we check it early.
When was that allocation recorded in your books and records?
Which forms did you receive for the year? 2025 is the first year Form 1099-DA exists. Reporting does not depend on receiving a form.
Did any Form 1099-DA you received show your cost basis? The IRS states that most 2025 statements will not provide basis, and that basis must be calculated before the 2025 return can be filed.
Do you already use crypto tax software?
What records can you provide? * Public wallet addresses and read-only CSV exports only.
Do you have acquisition dates and cost for the coins you sold?
Are there coins in your wallets whose original purchase you cannot document?
Are any prior year returns still unfiled?
Do you believe any prior return understated or left out crypto activity? Form 1040-X amends a return already filed. We ask so a preparer can look at it with you.
How did you answer the digital asset question on your most recent filed return? It is the question on page 1 of Form 1040, directly under Filing Status.
Have you received any IRS notice about digital assets?
If you have the letter to hand, the notice number identifies it.
Are you currently under examination, in Appeals, or in litigation on any tax matter?
Do you also need estimated tax or quarterly payment planning for the current year?
Do you hold crypto in a financial account outside the United States, or a foreign account holding anything besides crypto? We ask so a preparer can check with you whether FinCEN Form 114 (the FBAR) or Form 8938 comes into scope. Your answer here decides nothing on its own.
